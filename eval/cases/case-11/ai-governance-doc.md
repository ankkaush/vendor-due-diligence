# Merrow Cloud — AI Governance Documentation

**Version:** 1.4
**Published:** 2025-08-25

## Model Training Practices

Merrow does not use customer content to train foundational or third-party
AI models. Any AI-assisted features rely on models operated by Merrow
under contract terms that prohibit training on customer content.

## Data Handling for AI Features

Requests processed by AI-assisted features are logged for a period of 14
days for debugging and abuse-monitoring purposes, then deleted.

# Redmoor Systems — Data Processing Addendum

**Effective date:** 2025-04-01

## Section 4 — Data Retention

Upon termination, Redmoor shall delete all customer personal data within
**30 days**. No exceptions to this deletion timeline are specified in
this Addendum.

## Section 6 — Security Measures

Redmoor maintains encryption at rest and in transit for all customer
personal data.

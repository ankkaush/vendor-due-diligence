# Winnow Technologies — AI Governance Documentation

**Version:** 1.0
**Published:** 2025-08-01

## Responsible AI Statement

Winnow is committed to responsible AI development and implements
appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure the security
and integrity of any AI-assisted features on our platform.

## Model Training

Winnow does not offer AI-assisted features at this time and does not
operate or train any machine learning models on customer data.

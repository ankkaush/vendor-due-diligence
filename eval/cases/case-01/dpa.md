# Northwind Cloud Systems — Data Processing Addendum

**Effective date:** 2025-01-15

## Section 4 — Data Retention

Upon termination or expiration of the underlying services agreement,
Northwind shall delete or return all customer personal data within
**30 days**, except where retention is required by applicable law.

## Section 5 — Sub-processors

Northwind may engage sub-processors to assist in providing the services.
Northwind shall impose data protection obligations on each sub-processor
that are substantially equivalent to those set out in this Addendum, and
shall remain liable for each sub-processor's performance of those
obligations. A current list of sub-processors is available on request.

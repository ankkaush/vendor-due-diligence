# Solace Data Partners — Data Processing Addendum

**Effective date:** 2025-03-01

## Section 4 — Data Retention and Deletion

Upon termination or expiration of the underlying services agreement,
Solace shall delete all customer personal data within **30 days** of the
effective termination date. No exceptions to this deletion timeline are
specified in this Addendum.

## Section 6 — Security Measures

Solace maintains industry-standard technical and organizational measures
to protect customer personal data, including encryption in transit and at
rest, and role-based access controls.
